Admissions Process

Five simple steps to become part of our school family.

1

Inquiry

Submit an inquiry or schedule a campus tour.

2

Application

Complete the online application form and submit required documents.

3

Assessment

Student completes an age-appropriate assessment and interview.

4

Decision

Admissions committee reviews and sends a decision within 2 weeks.

5

Enrollment

Accept offer, pay deposit, and complete registration.

Requirements by Level

Elementary (K-5)

  • •Completed application form
  • •Birth certificate
  • •Previous school records
  • •Health/immunization records
  • •Two recent photographs

Middle School (6-8)

  • •Completed application form
  • •Birth certificate
  • •Previous 2 years of transcripts
  • •Standardized test scores
  • •Teacher recommendation letter
  • •Health/immunization records

High School (9-12)

  • •Completed application form
  • •Birth certificate
  • •Complete high school transcript
  • •SAT/ACT scores
  • •Two teacher recommendation letters
  • •Personal statement/essay
  • •Extracurricular activities list
